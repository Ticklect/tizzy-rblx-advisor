# Monetization brain

## Health check first

Before optimizing revenue, determine whether the game is healthy enough to monetize harder. Strong retention + weak monetization is often more fixable than strong extraction + weak retention.

Never blindly prescribe more paywalls/gamepasses.

## Research -> hypothesis -> update -> results

Use:
1. sort product sales / purchase timing;
2. observe live players;
3. read complaints/desires;
4. study comparable games;
5. inspect economy pacing/sinks;
6. form product/pathway hypothesis;
7. ship/test;
8. inspect revenue **and** retention/playtime/D1/D7/FTUE;
9. keep, revert or iterate.

## Pain/desire mapping

Strong offers often solve an existing player desire/pain:
- effort/grind/time
- protection/risk
- convenience
- status/flex/expression
- scarcity/FOMO
- missed offline value
- progression acceleration
- high-intent "I almost have enough" moments

Do not deliberately make the base experience miserable just to sell relief.

## Expand what already works

If one category sells well, ask why. The lesson is the underlying desire, not merely that SKU. Test tiers/variants carefully where appropriate.

## Consumable vs permanent

If the value naturally expires/repeats, a dev product/consumable may fit repeat spend better than a permanent entitlement. If the value is permanent, a pass may be more coherent. Current Roblox product types/features should be verified.

## Purchase pathways

Conversion can fail because the right offer is hard to discover. Pathways may include:
- HUD/shop access
- contextual low-currency state
- world placement at high-traffic points
- relevant moment immediately after the need appears

Avoid spam. The corpus explicitly warns that aggressive prompts can hurt playtime and player sentiment.

## Economy guardrail

Speed/cash/earnings/luck boosts can compress progression and exhaust content. Model the downstream economy before raising value or lowering price.

## Placement and timing

Offer value at the moment the desire is salient. Offline earnings doubling at claim time is stronger than an unrelated popup at spawn. Protection offer near the protected asset/context is easier to understand.

## Monetization output

Healthy loop? -> top products/behaviors -> player pain/desire -> pathway/placement -> offer design -> economy risk -> experiment -> revenue metric + retention/playtime guardrails.
